Descriptions for each column in the aggregated ride-sharing data CSV:

1. request_datetime_floored: The pickup request time rounded down to the nearest hour.
hvfhs_license_num: The High-Volume For-Hire Service license number that identifies
the ride-sharing service provider. These are companies like Uber (HV0003) and Lyft
(HV0005).

2. PULocationID: Pickup Location ID that corresponds to specific taxi zones within NYC.
These IDs map to geographic areas defined by the NYC Taxi & Limousine Commission
(see lookup table).

3. trip_miles: The average distance traveled for trips in this grouping, measured in miles.

4. trip_time: The average duration of trips in this grouping, measured in seconds.

5. base_passenger_fare: The average amount charged to passengers (excluding tolls,
surcharges, and tips) for trips in this grouping.

6. tolls: The average amount of tolls paid during trips in this grouping.

7. congestion_surcharge: The average congestion surcharge applied to trips in this
grouping. This fee is charged for trips that enter Manhattan's congestion zone.

8. airport_fee: The average fee charged for trips that start or end at airports in this
grouping.

9. tips: The average tip amount given by passengers for trips in this grouping.

10. driver_pay: The average amount paid to drivers for trips in this grouping.

11. request_hour: The hour of the day (0-23) when the ride was requested, extracted from
the request_datetime_floored field.

12. request_date: The calendar date when the ride was requested, extracted from the
request_datetime_floored field.
